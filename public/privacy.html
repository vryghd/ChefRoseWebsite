Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Very Ghood ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at this domain in connection with our food ordering and catering services ("Dinner with Chef Rose"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. By using our site you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you place a menu order or submit a catering inquiry, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (catering bookings)
  • Event details you provide (date, guest count, event type, special requests)
  • Order details including items selected, side choices, and special instructions

Payment Information

We do not collect, store, or process your credit or debit card details directly. All online payments — including the $250 catering deposit and menu order payments — are processed by Stripe, Inc. Stripe collects and handles payment data under their own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy. We receive only a transaction confirmation and, where applicable, the last four digits of your card.

Automatically Collected Information

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare may collect standard web server log data at the infrastructure level, including IP addresses, browser type, and pages requested, for security and performance purposes. This is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. We do not independently log or analyze this data beyond what Cloudflare provides for site management.

Local Storage (Your Browser)

We use your browser's localStorage to maintain your shopping cart between page views. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us until you place an order. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Fulfilling orders: We use your name, phone, and order details to prepare and coordinate pickup or catering delivery.
  • Pickup coordination: We send the pickup address via the phone number you provide after your order is confirmed.
  • Catering coordination: We use your contact details and event information to confirm your booking, communicate logistics, and process your deposit.
  • Service communications: We may contact you about your order status, changes, or follow-up. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.
  • Legal obligations: We retain order records as required for tax and business accounting purposes.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Stripe: Payment processing, as described above.
  • Google LLC: Our menu is managed through Google Sheets. Order data is not stored in Google Sheets; only menu content (item names, descriptions, prices, and available sides) is maintained there.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.

4. Data Retention

We retain your order information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the order and meet our accounting and tax obligations, typically no longer than three (3) years. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us (see Section 8).

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

6. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at rose@veryghood.com.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI DSS compliant. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us:

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
